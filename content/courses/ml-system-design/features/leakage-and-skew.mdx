---
title: "5. Correctness: leakage and skew"
description: The two most common feature bugs in production.
---

## 5.1 Target leakage / time-travel leakage

A feature contains the label, or data from after the prediction time. The model looks great offline and fails live.

## 5.2 Training-serving skew

Code, data or timing differs between training and serving, so the model sees different inputs in production.

## 5.3 Log-and-wait

Log the exact feature values used at serving time and train on those logs, which removes skew by construction.
