---
title: 6. Selection and evaluation
description: Deciding which features are worth keeping.
---

## 6.1 Importance and ablation

Measure importance with permutation or SHAP, and confirm with ablations: remove a feature, retrain, compare.

## 6.2 Redundancy and collinearity

Features that carry the same information add cost without gain and make importance hard to read.

## 6.3 Cost-benefit per feature

Weigh each feature's gain against its latency, compute and maintenance cost.
