---
title: F. Risk and compliance
description: What the system must never do.
---

## F1 Privacy, PII, regulation

Identify personal data and the rules that apply (GDPR, PCI DSS). This limits which features you can use at all.

## F2 Fairness

Check whether decisions could harm protected groups, and decide which fairness metrics to track.

## F3 Explainability and audit

Some decisions need stated reasons, such as declines. Keep records so any decision can be audited later.

## F4 Adversaries and abuse

In fraud and abuse, attackers probe the system and adapt. Plan for fast policy changes and monitoring.
