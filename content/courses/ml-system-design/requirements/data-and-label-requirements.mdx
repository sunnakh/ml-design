---
title: E. Data and label requirements
description: What must exist to learn anything.
---

## E1 Data available at prediction time

Only data that exists at the moment of prediction can be a feature. Anything known later causes leakage.

## E2 Label source, quality, delay

Identify where labels come from, how noisy they are, and how long they take. Fraud chargebacks arrive 30–90 days late, and declined payments never get a label.

## E3 Volume and class imbalance

Check there are enough examples, especially positives. At 0.3% fraud, most data is negative, which affects sampling, metrics and training.
