---
title: G. Requirements process
description: How to gather, negotiate and write down requirements.
---

## G1 Clarifying questions

Ask about users, scale, latency, batch vs real-time, the business goal and any existing baseline before designing.

## G2 Back-of-envelope estimation

Turn answers into numbers: QPS, storage, positives per day, review volume. Rough math catches impossible designs early.

## G3 Trade-off negotiation

Stakeholders want conflicting things. Make the trade-offs explicit, ideally in one currency, and get agreement.

## G4 Writing the spec, revisiting it

Write requirements down as a spec everyone signs off on, and revisit it when the world or the business changes.
