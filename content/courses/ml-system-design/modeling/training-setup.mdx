---
title: 4. Training setup
description: Everything that decides what the model actually learns.
---

## 4.1 Loss functions

Match the loss to the task: log loss for probabilities, pairwise or listwise losses for ranking, regression losses for amounts.

## 4.2 Splits and leakage

Split by time or entity, not randomly, so validation reflects future traffic.

## 4.3 Imbalance, negative sampling

Rare positives need resampling, reweighting or sampled negatives. Correct the probabilities afterwards.

## 4.4 Bias in logged data

Logs only contain reactions to items you showed. Position and selection bias need correction or exploration data.

## 4.5 Calibration

Make predicted probabilities match observed rates (Platt scaling, isotonic regression), especially when scores are multiplied or thresholded.
