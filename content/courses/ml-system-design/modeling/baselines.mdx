title: 2. Baselines
description: Start simple so you know what "better" means.
---

## 2.1 Heuristic baselines

Rules such as "most popular items" or "recent purchases" are cheap and often surprisingly strong.

## 2.2 Simple ML baselines

Logistic regression or a small tree model with basic features sets the bar a complex model must clear.

## 2.3 Baseline as sanity check

If a complex model barely beats the baseline, check for bugs, leakage or a weak signal before adding complexity.
