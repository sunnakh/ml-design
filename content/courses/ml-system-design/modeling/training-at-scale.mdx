---
title: 5. Training at scale
description: Training when data or models outgrow one machine.
---

## 5.1 Data parallelism

Split batches across many workers, each holding a full model copy, and combine gradients.

## 5.2 Model parallelism, big embeddings

Split large models or huge embedding tables across devices when they don't fit on one.

## 5.3 Hardware, checkpointing

Pick CPUs, GPUs or accelerators for cost and speed, and checkpoint regularly so failures don't lose progress.
