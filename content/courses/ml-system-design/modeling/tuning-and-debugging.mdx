---
title: 6. Tuning and debugging
description: Finding out why a model underperforms and fixing it.
---

## 6.1 Bias–variance diagnosis

Compare training and validation error to tell underfitting from overfitting, and choose the fix accordingly.

## 6.2 Hyperparameter search

Random search, Bayesian optimization or early-stopping methods find good settings within a compute budget.

## 6.3 Error analysis, slicing

Look at errors by segment and by hand to find what to fix next: data, features or labels.

## 6.4 Tracking, reproducibility

Log every run's data, code, parameters and metrics so results can be compared and reproduced.
