---
title: 7. Retraining and evolution
description: Keeping the model current after launch.
---

## 7.1 Retrain cadence

Scheduled, triggered by drift, or continuous. Match it to how fast the world changes.

## 7.2 Cold start

New users and items have no history. Use content features, popularity or exploration until data arrives.

## 7.3 Continual learning

Update the model incrementally on fresh data. Fresher, but riskier and harder to validate.

## 7.4 Replacing a live model

Validate offline, shadow, canary and A/B test before a new model takes over, with a fast rollback path.
