---
title: 6. Release and rollout
description: Getting a new model to users safely.
---

## 6.1 Shadow mode

Run the new model on live traffic without serving its outputs, and compare.

## 6.2 Canary

Send 1–5% of traffic to the new model and watch errors and key metrics.

## 6.3 A/B testing

Split traffic to measure the business impact before full rollout.

## 6.4 Blue-green, rollback

Keep the old version ready so you can switch back instantly.

## 6.5 Guardrail metrics

Automatically halt a rollout if latency, errors or business guardrails get worse.
