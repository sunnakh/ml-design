Deployment answers three questions: **where and when** the model runs (prediction mode, cloud vs edge), **how fast** it runs (latency optimization, scaling), and **how to release it safely** (rollout, packaging and CI/CD).

<Diagram src="deployment/deployment-subtopics.svg" />

## Prediction mode comes first

| Mode | How it works | Use when | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batch | Precompute predictions on a schedule and store them | Inputs are known ahead and results can be hours old | Nightly email recommendations |
| Online | Compute a prediction per request | Inputs are new at request time and you need a fast response | Search ranking, fraud check at payment |
| Streaming | Features from an event stream plus online inference | The last few seconds of behavior matter | Feed ranking after each click |

Many real systems are hybrids: batch-generate candidates, then re-rank online.

## Running example: PayFlow fraud detection

When someone taps their card, the payment network allows about 300 ms end to end. The fraud check gets **50 ms of that (p99)** and returns approve, decline, or step-up (SMS code).

- **Model:** gradient-boosted trees, trained on 2 years of transactions.
- **Labels:** chargebacks, arriving 30–90 days later.
- **Features:** amount, merchant category, 90-day average spend, transactions in the last 10 minutes, distance from last transaction, device fingerprint.
- **Volume:** 5,000 transactions per second at peak.
