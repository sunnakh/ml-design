description: Why offline wins often fail online.

## D1 Proxy gap

The offline metric is a proxy. Improving it may not move the business metric.

## D2 Feedback loops

Logs reflect the old system's choices, so offline evaluation favors models that mimic it.

## D3 Off-policy evaluation

Estimate how a new policy would have performed from logs of the old one, using techniques like inverse propensity scoring.
