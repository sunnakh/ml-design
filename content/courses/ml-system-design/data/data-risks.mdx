---
title: 7. Data risks
description: The failures interviewers look for.
---

## 7.1 Leakage

A feature contains information about the label that won't exist when the model predicts. The classic cause is a split that isn't time-based.

## 7.2 Train-serve skew

Features are computed one way in training and another in serving. A feature store fixes this.

## 7.3 Bias & feedback loops

Selection bias, and loops where the model only learns from items it chose to show.

## 7.4 Distribution shift

Inputs (covariate shift), label rates (label shift) or the input–label relationship (concept shift) change over time.
