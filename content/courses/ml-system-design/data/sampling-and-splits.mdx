---
title: 6. Sampling, imbalance & splits
description: Choosing which examples to train and test on.
---

## 6.1 Sampling methods

Stratified, reservoir, importance and negative sampling each control which examples end up in the dataset.

## 6.2 Class imbalance

With 0.1% fraud, downsample the majority class, upweight the minority, or use focal loss, and pick metrics that reflect imbalance.

## 6.3 Temporal & entity-based splits

Split by time when data is time-ordered, and by entity (user, merchant) when examples from one entity are related. Random splits leak information.
