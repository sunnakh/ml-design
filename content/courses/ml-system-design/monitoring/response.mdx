---
title: 7. Response
description: What to do when something breaks.
---

## 7.1 Diagnosis

Start at the bottom of the stack: system, then data quality, then drift, then model quality.

## 7.2 Rollback

Return to the last good model or config quickly while you investigate.

## 7.3 Retraining

Retrain on fresh data on a schedule, on a trigger, or continuously. For some concept drift, go back to problem framing instead.
